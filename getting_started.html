Getting Started with Crochet

Welcome to the basics of crochet! This page will help you understand the materials and techniques you'll need to begin your journey. Starting a new journey always seems daunting, but with the right guidance, you'll be creating beautiful projects in no time.

Materials You'll Need

When starting it's good to have more than just a yarn and hook. You'll also need things like stitch markers and tapestry needles to help with your projects. Check out the Tools & Materials page for more details.

How to Read a Crochet Pattern

Crochet patterns look confusing at first, but they follow simple rules. Here's what you need to know:

  • Abbreviations: ch, sc, dc, sl st, etc.
  • Rows vs Rounds: Rows go back and forth; rounds go in circles.
  • Gauge: Measures how big your stitches are — important for sizing.
  • Repeats: Often shown using parentheses or asterisks.

Basic Techniques

Start out by learning the foundational stitches like chain stitch, single crochet, and double crochet. Practice these until you feel comfortable. These form the basis for most crochet patterns, the more you practice it will be like second nature.
